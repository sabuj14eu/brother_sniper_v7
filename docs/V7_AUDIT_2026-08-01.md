excursion (undecided 14→25). Caveat: 60s sampling and only 1 TP-hit among
decided cases make avgR loser-dominated — but the direction is unambiguous:
**tight stops lose because they are attached to bad trades, not because they
are tight.** This settles the CLAUDE.md question in favor of trade SELECTION
levers (asset gate, counter-trend penalty) over stop-width changes. n=47,
above MIN_N but below the ~100-trade judgment bar — re-run at n≥100.
